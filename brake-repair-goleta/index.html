Signs your brakes need attention

  • Squealing, grinding or scraping when you stop
  • A soft or spongy pedal, or a pedal that sinks
  • The car pulls to one side under braking
  • Vibration or pulsing through the pedal or steering wheel
  • A brake warning light on the dash

Common questions

How do I know if I need new brakes?

Noise, a soft pedal, pulling or vibration are the usual signs. If you are not sure, bring it in and we will take a look and tell you what we see.
