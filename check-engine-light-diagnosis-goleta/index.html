Common reasons the light comes on

  • Loose or faulty gas cap
  • Oxygen sensor or catalytic converter problems
  • Ignition or spark plug issues
  • Mass airflow or other sensor faults
  • Evaporative emissions system leaks

Common questions

Is it safe to drive with the check engine light on?

If the light is steady, you can usually drive a short distance to get it checked. If it flashes, or the car runs rough, stop driving and call us.

Is a code reader enough to diagnose the problem?

A code points to a system, not always to the failed part. We confirm the cause before recommending a repair.

Do you diagnose Teslas and EVs?

We service Teslas and other electric vehicles. Call us to discuss your specific issue.
