Our story

Family-run since 2016.

Angel's Auto Repair started in 2016 as a small, family-owned shop built on trust, hard work, and a passion for quality automotive care. As the community grew with us, we moved into a larger facility to keep up.

It is still run by Angel and his two sons, Alan and Angel Jr. When you drop off your car, you deal with the family that fixes it, and you get honest service and personal attention.

Our mission is simple: reliable, high-quality repairs at a fair price, so our neighbors stay safe on the road.

Cars have always been a big part of our family's life, and that passion drives everything we do.

Alan T., owner
